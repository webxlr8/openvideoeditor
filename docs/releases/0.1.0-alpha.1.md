# OpenVideoEditor 0.1.0 alpha 1

Release tag `v0.1.0-alpha.1`; the app itself reports version 0.1.0 (Windows installers require a numeric version).

First alpha for invited testers. It is unsigned, untested on many machines, and will have rough edges. Your projects
are saved as durable revisions after every edit, but keep copies of anything you can't lose.

## Install

| Platform | Requirement | File |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS | 14 or later, Apple silicon (M1 or newer) | `OpenVideoEditor_0.1.0_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows | Windows 11, x64 | `OpenVideoEditor_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` |

The installers are not signed yet. If a platform is missing from this release, it is listed under "Not in this
build" below and follows in a later build.

Every installer is checked automatically before it is published, on a fresh machine of its platform:

- **Windows:** the installer (built on Linux with the MinGW-w64 toolchain) is installed silently on Windows Server
  2025 (build 26100, the same as Windows 11 24H2). Its command-line tool then imports H.264, HEVC 10-bit, AV1, ProRes,
  VP9 and AAC media, grabs frames, edits the timeline as text with undo, analyses audio, exports MP4 twice,
  round-trips the timeline through FCPXML, XML, EDL and OTIO, and answers as an MCP server. The app is started, must
  stay up without errors, and must show a test clip's picture in its viewer on the real screen.
- **macOS:** the same checks run on the command-line tool inside the app and on the app itself, on a macOS 15
  Apple-silicon machine.
- **The app's screens:** a development build of the app is driven through 15 flows on Windows and Linux (import,
  timeline editing, inspector, text, captions, effects and transitions, export, templates, the assistant against a
  scripted stand-in, the generation confirm step, settings, close and reopen, kill and relaunch). The tool that
  drives the screens does not exist for macOS, so the Mac app's screens are not clicked through.

These are virtual machines without real GPUs, so testers on real Macs and PCs are still especially valuable this
round.

- **macOS:** open the DMG and drag the app to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**,
  then **Open** again. If macOS says the app is damaged, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
  /Applications/OpenVideoEditor.app` in Terminal.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What you can do

- **Edit:** import footage, then cut, trim, split, move and stack clips on a multi-track timeline, with undo, redo
  and a full **History** you can restore from.
- **Style:** titles with 16 animations; 41 effects; 24 transitions; 15 layouts; colour wheels, curves and LUTs.
- **Captions:** made on your computer (speech models download on first use) in 21 styles, with a transcript editor.
- **Motion graphics:** sandboxed code items, with 8 templates to start from.
- **Assistant** (bring your own Anthropic API key):
  - It edits the same timeline you see.
  - Each turn shows what it changed, with **Show** and **Undo this turn**.
  - Costs are shown per turn, chat and project.
  - Spend caps are in Settings → Assistant.
- **Generate** images, video, voice, music and sound effects with your own fal.ai, OpenAI or ElevenLabs keys. The
  cost shows first, and nothing is spent until you confirm.
- **Export:** MP4 (H.264 + AAC) at 720p, 1080p or 4K. Export never overwrites your previous file unless the new one
  finished.
- **Exchange timelines** with Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro (FCPXML, XML, EDL, OpenTimelineIO). The
  dialog lists anything that won't carry over before it writes.
- **Agents and scripts:** `ove` command line and MCP server for Claude Code, Codex and other agents
  (`ove skills install`).

## Privacy

- No account, no sign-in, no telemetry.
- Media never leaves your computer.
- The assistant sends the timeline text, your chat and tool results to Anthropic; never media files or frames.
- Generation sends your prompt and any reference image you attach to the provider you pick.
- Keys are kept in the system keychain and are redacted from logs.
- Crash reports stay on your computer unless you choose to copy one into a bug report.

## Known limitations

- **Speed:**
  - Timeline preview is rendered on the CPU at 540p and 30 fps. Heavy effects or several 4K streams may stutter.
  - There are no proxies yet.
- **Hardware:**
  - The timeline preview does not use hardware decoding yet; the single-clip viewer does.
  - Performance has not been measured on reference Macs and PCs yet.
- **HDR** (HLG/PQ, such as iPhone footage): it is shown and exported tone-mapped to SDR, assuming a 1000-nit peak. HDR
  export is not available.
- **Export formats:**
  - MP4 with H.264 only.
  - Whole-number frame rates (24–60), so no 23.976 or 29.97.
  - If no hardware H.264 encoder is found, export falls back to a lower-quality codec and tells you.
- **Not in this alpha:**
  - Subject masks: "subject" and "background" effect regions apply to the whole frame, and behind-subject captions
    draw in front.
  - Auto-reframe that follows a person (vertical crops are centred and adjustable).
  - Silence and filler-word removal.
  - Loudness normalisation.
  - Noise reduction.
  - Speaker labels in captions.
  - Stock media.
  - Voice cloning.
  - In-place updates.
- **Interchange** does not carry captions, motion graphics, effects, keyframes or transitions. The export dialog lists
  them.
- **Windows:**
  - Export uses Media Foundation's H.264 encoder (hardware when available) and falls back to a lower-quality codec
    with a warning.
  - The preview is drawn inside the app's page, not directly by the GPU as on macOS (that viewer does not show the
    picture on Windows yet), so playback may be less smooth at large window sizes.
  - Hardware decoding (D3D11VA) has only been run on virtual machines.
- **Linux** builds exist for development only.

## Open-source components

The app includes FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1), dav1d, zlib, whisper.cpp, React, Tauri and many Rust crates. Their licences are
in the app's `licenses` folder. The FFmpeg source and the scripts we build it with are attached to this release.

## Reporting problems

Use **Send feedback** on Home: it builds a report with your app version, OS and GPU (and the latest crash report if you
turned that on in Settings → Privacy) for you to copy. Send it in reply to your invitation, or open an issue in the
repository if you have access. Include:

- what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
- your OS version and Mac or PC model.
