# Proposal: add-effects-and-color

## Why

Looks, transitions and layouts turn cuts into finished videos.

## What Changes

- 41 effects with parameter contracts (CPU), per item and as shader items.
- 24 transitions with duration, easing and direction; 15 layouts.
- Colour grade: wheels, curves, saturation, .cube LUTs (nine generated looks, CC0), match to a reference frame, histogram scope.
